👋 **Welcome！**

I am a final-year PhD candidate in the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering
at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, advised by
[Prof. Hailong Huang](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/en/aae/people/academic-staff/dr-huang-hailong/).
My research focuses on tethered aerial robots for aerial physical interaction, spanning
robust control, trajectory planning, and multi-robot cooperation.

Previously, I received my B.Eng. from [Xiamen University](https://en.xmu.edu.cn/) in 2019,
advised by [Prof. Liaoni Wu](https://aerospace.xmu.edu.cn/info/2163/49693.htm),
and my M.Eng. from [Beihang University](https://ev.buaa.edu.cn/) in 2021,
advised by [Prof. Jia Song](https://shi.buaa.edu.cn/songjia/).

I also worked as a Control Algorithm Engineer at XPENG AEROHT from January 2022
to September 2023. Read more about my [industry and project experience](/experience/).

I am open to discussion and collaboration. Feel free to reach out.

My research interests:

- Tethered aerial robots for aerial physical interaction
- Robust control and estimation
- Trajectory planning for UAVs

You can find my publications on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=B4ECVkEAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN).
